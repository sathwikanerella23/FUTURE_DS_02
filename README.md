# 📊 Customer Retention & Business Churn Analytics

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns and retention trends to understand customer behavior and improve business decision-making. The analysis helps identify factors influencing customer churn and provides insights to enhance customer retention strategies.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed

- Customer Churn Distribution Analysis
- Contract-wise Churn Analysis
- Gender-based Customer Analysis
- Tenure & Retention Trend Analysis
- Customer Behavior Visualization

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customers with shorter tenure showed higher churn rates.
- Month-to-month contract customers were more likely to leave.
- Long-term customers demonstrated better retention.
- Electronic check payment users showed comparatively higher churn.
- Customer retention improved with longer subscription periods.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Encourage customers to choose long-term subscription plans.
- Improve onboarding and engagement strategies.
- Identify at-risk customers at an early stage.
- Provide retention offers and loyalty benefits.
- Enhance customer support and service quality.
